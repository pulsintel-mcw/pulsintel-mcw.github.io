About MyCaseWatch

MyCaseWatch is an intelligent case-tracking platform that helps users monitor government case progress—starting with USCIS case status updates.

What we do

We connect to the official USCIS Case Status API to present timely status checks, simplified timelines, and optional alerts for authorized users.

Who we are

MyCaseWatch is a product operated by Pulsintel LLC, a company incorporated in the State of Maryland, USA. We emphasize security, transparency, and responsible data handling in all aspects of the platform.
